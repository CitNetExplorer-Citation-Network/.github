## CitNetExplorer Highlights

- **Time On The Vertical Axis:** CitNetExplorer publication tree lays citing and cited papers from an export you ran so lineage is visible year by year.
- **Chains, Not Word Clouds:** CitNetExplorer citation chain follows who cites whom in a corpus you exported, which a term map will not.
- **Drill Into A Lineage:** CitNetExplorer paper lineage can expand a cluster of works you selected from that set.
- **CWTS Sibling To VOSviewer:** CitNetExplorer citation network is the genealogy desk; VOSviewer is the landscape desk.
- **Not Author Metrics:** H-index and similar scores belong in Publish or Perish.

## What Is CitNetExplorer

CitNetExplorer is a Windows tool from CWTS for exploring citation networks of publications. After a Web of Science-style export you are allowed to use, you open CitNetExplorer citation network to see how ideas hop forward in time. CitNetExplorer publication tree is the picture. CitNetExplorer citation chain is a path of citing works. CitNetExplorer paper lineage is the subset you expand when a review needs ancestors and descendants of a seed paper you chose.

VOSviewer would show a co-citation landscape of the same field. CitNetExplorer asks a different question: which papers sit on the spine of this topic across years? Pajek could analyze a huge citation graph after conversion; this seat is built for the bibliometric export and the genealogy view.

Use corpora from searches you ran or files a librarian delivered for a review you write. Respect license terms of the database. Do not republish a full proprietary export as an open dataset if the terms forbid it.

## Starting with CitNetExplorer

Prerequisites: A PC you administer and a citation export from a search you are allowed to keep.

1. **Prepare:** Export cited references with the fields CitNetExplorer citation network expects, from the account you hold.
2. **Configure:** Load that corpus and set the year window for the review you write.
3. **Test:** Locate a seed paper you know and confirm CitNetExplorer paper lineage shows it.
4. **Refine:** Expand CitNetExplorer citation chain around that seed, dropping noise years.
5. **Apply:** Screenshot or export CitNetExplorer publication tree for the manuscript, with database and date.
6. **Maintain:** Rerun when the search updates, and keep the raw export beside the project.

## Best Uses

- **Literature Review Spine:** CitNetExplorer publication tree of a topic you were asked to cover.
- **Ancestor Hunt For A Method You Use:** CitNetExplorer paper lineage from a seed in your export.
- **Teaching How Citations Flow:** A public WoS teaching extract the course already licensed.
- **Not A Term Landscape:** Use VOSviewer for words.
- **Not An H-Index Report:** Use Publish or Perish.
- **Not A Survey Matrix:** Use UCINET or SocNetV.
- **One Topic Per Project:** Do not mix two fields in one CitNetExplorer citation network.
- **Check The Original PDF You Have Access To:** Chains can include papers you then read.
- **Caption The Database:** CitNetExplorer citation chain should say which index you queried.
- **Seed From A Paper You Actually Cite:** Lineage is only useful if the seed is in your review.
- **Year Window Honest To The Question:** Do not hide early work by clipping CitNetExplorer publication tree.
- **Keep Proprietary Exports Closed:** Share figures, not a dump the license forbids.
- **Drop Isolated Items After You Check Them:** CitNetExplorer citation network should not hide a seed you still need.
- **Forward And Backward From The Same Seed:** CitNetExplorer paper lineage in both directions when the review asks.
- **Compare Two Seeds, Not Two Unrelated Fields:** CitNetExplorer citation chain is a spine, not a dump.
- **Screenshot At A Readable Year Scale:** CitNetExplorer publication tree labels must survive print.
